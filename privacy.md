# Zola — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Zola is a personal assistant used only by its developer. It is not available to other users.

**What it accesses.** With the owner's permission, Zola reads his own Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive files and Google Contacts, and creates and sends Gmail drafts. Every email is sent only after the owner explicitly approves it. Zola does not delete, archive or label email.

**Background monitoring.** When the owner enables it, Zola periodically checks his Gmail and Google Calendar for new or changed items so it can tell him about things that matter, including replies he is waiting for. This runs on the owner's own computer and only reads; it never sends, changes or deletes anything.

**Where data goes.** Google data is fetched directly from Google to the owner's own Windows computer. To answer requests, and to decide which new items are worth mentioning, limited parts of that data (such as sender, subject and a short preview, and, for background ranking, a shortened body (see Ranking below) are sent to the AI model provider the owner uses (OpenAI), under his account and its data settings. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

**Speech. When voice replies are on, the text of Zola’s spoken replies is sent to Deepgram to be turned into audio. That text may include details from the owner’s email or calendar that Zola is answering about. Nothing else is sent to Deepgram.

**Ranking. Zola reads new mail in the background and sends a trimmed copy to OpenAI through the owner’s ChatGPT account: who it appears to be from, the sender’s domain, the subject, and a shortened body. OpenAI returns a label and a one- or two-sentence summary. Those are stored on the owner’s computer, and the body is deleted after that. Training is off. Asking Zola to forget something removes it from Zola’s own store. It does not pull back a copy OpenAI already received.

**Storage.** Sign-in credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. Conversation history and the list of items Zola is tracking are stored locally on that computer and can be deleted by the owner.

**Removing access.** The owner can revoke Zola's access at any time from his Google Account (Security → Third-party apps and services).
